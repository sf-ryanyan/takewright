# Genre: short-form social (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, 抖音, 小红书)

## Format
9:16, 15–45 s, shots of 3–6 s, one idea per shot, captions added in the edit (bottom third safe area kept clear in generation).

## The first 2 seconds (hook patterns)
- **Slow reveal** — tight detail → slow pull-back to the full subject.
- **Material close-up** — macro texture with raking light.
- **Negative space** — small subject in a big calm frame, gentle push-in.
- **Reflection** — subject seen in glass, water or lacquer.
- **Shadow entrance** — light slowly reveals the subject.
- **Pattern interrupt** — an unexpected but safe motion in the very first frame.
- **Before → after** — split or wipe between two states (both real).
Premium brands: the first five. Energetic or educational content: pattern interrupt or before/after. When budget allows, draft two hook variants and keep the stronger.

## Structure (30 s)
hook (0–2 s) → promise (2–5 s) → 3–4 value shots (5–25 s) → payoff + call to action with space for text (25–30 s).

## Rules
- Movement in every shot, but one move per shot.
- Pace in the edit, not in the prompt: generate clean 4–6 s shots, cut them shorter.
- Voiceover pace: ~2.5 English words or ~4 Chinese characters per second.
- Use generated or licensed music (`assembly/editing.md` for ducking under voice).
- Export 9:16 first; 1:1 and 16:9 variants from the same edit if needed.
