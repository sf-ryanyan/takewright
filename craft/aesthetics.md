# Aesthetics library

Choose one coherent look per project and write it once as a **Look Card** (palette, light, lens, grade, texture, sound). Reuse the same words in every shot.

## Premium look rules (default for brand, product, property and lifestyle work)
- One subject; no clutter.
- 40–60% negative space.
- Slow camera only (no whip pans); hold shots slightly longer than feels comfortable.
- Restrained, low-saturation palette; one accent colour at most.
- Show materials up close — wood grain, stone veining, fabric weave, brushed metal.
- Quiet sound — ambience or a single instrument; no drums.
- Restraint over effects; end on a calm hold.

## Lighting
soft key (calm, commercial) · window light (natural, intimate) · backlight / rim light (separation, premium edge on glass and metal) · golden hour (warm, aspirational) · blue hour with warm interior lamps (evening exteriors) · high key (clean, bright, beauty) · low key (drama, suspense) · motivated practicals (lamps, screens, candles with a visible source) · top light (harsh, institutional).

## Lens feel
16–24 mm wide (interiors, scale — keep verticals straight) · 35 mm (natural, lifestyle, narrative) · 50 mm (balanced portrait and product) · 85 mm (portrait isolation, compression) · 100 mm macro (materials, food, jewellery, eyes). Shallow focus isolates the subject; deep focus keeps rooms and blocking readable; a rack focus shifts attention between two details.

## Colour grade
clean neutral (architecture, e-commerce, tech) · warm golden (home, hospitality, nostalgia) · cool cyan shadows (tech, thriller, night) · desaturated earth (documentary realism) · pastel (beauty, wellness) · high-contrast monochrome (fashion, noir) · teal-and-orange (blockbuster energy — use sparingly).

## Texture
clean digital (architecture, tech, luxury) · fine film grain (organic, period) · halation / mist filter (dreamy, romantic) · crisp high shutter (action clarity) · motion blur (speed, energy).

## Optional anchors
- Camera/lens anchor, one only: "shot on ARRI Alexa with a 35 mm lens", "85 mm f/1.2 portrait lens", "Kodak 35 mm film look". Do not stack several.
- Handheld life (people and narrative only): "handheld, with an extremely subtle breath-like camera float". Never for property or product shots — use tripod, gimbal or dolly wording.

## Look presets

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial minimal | hard raking side light, geometric shadows | 50 mm, static or slow push | near-white with one muted accent | silence, one tone |

## Taste checks before submitting
- Does every element earn its place? Remove one thing.
- Is the light motivated (a believable source)?
- Is there room for titles/captions where the edit will need them?
- Would the frame still look good as a still photograph?
