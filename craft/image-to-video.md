# Image-to-video

The best control over identity, layout and look comes from animating an approved image (a real photo or a generated key frame).

## Core rule
Prompt only what the image cannot show. The image already holds identity, layout, materials, palette and composition; describing them again causes drift. Add motion, camera, light change, timing, sound and preservation locks.

Template:
`The reference image is the opening frame; preserve [layout / face / product / architecture] exactly. Only [motion] changes. Camera: [one move with an endpoint]. Light: [source or change]. Sound: [cue]. Do not [what must not change].`

## Choose a mode first
- **Hold mode** — the image *is* the moment (property, product, scenery, portrait). 2–4 micro-motions (curtain drift, sunlight sliding across the floor, steam, leaves, water shimmer, a blink, a breath). Camera static or one slow push-in. Double lock, positive + negative: "the room stays exactly the same; no furniture moves and no new objects appear."
- **React mode** — something happens to the subject (drama, people). Split one emotion into sub-beats with ≥2 s to land ("she hears the knock, her eyes lift, her shoulders tense over two seconds"). If the image is mid-scene, add "the clip begins exactly at this moment".

## Preservation locks (lock only what must stay fixed)
preserve face identity · preserve logo and label · preserve room layout and furniture · preserve window and door positions · preserve outfit and hairstyle · preserve product shape · no shape change · no new objects.

## First + last frame
Use when the shot must travel between two known states (exterior → interior, closed → open box). Both frames must share lighting and lens feel; describe the path between them, not the frames themselves.

## Good additions
| Add | Example |
|---|---|
| Micro-expression | the subject blinks once and lowers their eyes |
| Product light | a thin highlight travels across the label |
| Weather | rain streaks behind the subject; droplets bead on the glass |
| Camera | slow dolly-in from the current framing to the window |
| Atmosphere | dust catches the doorway beam and settles |
| Audio | soft room tone, one key click at the end |

## Fixes
- Identity drifts → less new description, stronger locks, less motion.
- Camera jumps → one move with start and end.
- Product warps → "preserved, static identity, no shape change".
- Output is static → add one physical action and one time cue.
- Background changes → lock the layout; animate only light, weather or atmosphere.
- Hands deform → simplify the hand action or keep hands out of the main action.

## Image quality first
A weak source image makes a weak video. For generated key frames: correct ratio from the start, clean composition, no text, consistent light. For real photos: level horizon, straight verticals, no motion blur, and at least the target resolution.
